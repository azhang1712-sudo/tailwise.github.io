Five quick steps, about two minutes. We match on how you live, never on a dog's age.

How matching works

Your fit score comes only from what you tell us and from each dog's temperament, size, care needs and estimated costs. A dog's age is never part of it. Your ZIP and radius decide which shelters are in range. When two dogs fit about equally, dogs with the fewest days left, then dogs who have waited longest, appear a little earlier. Anything a shelter has not assessed is shown as unknown, never assumed. Work hours set how long the dog would be alone. Sleep schedule, meals and your own words are passed to the shelter with your interest but are not scored yet.
